Last updated: October 8, 2026

PAT AUTOSELL is an e-commerce management application designed to help authorized users manage product listings, inventory, pricing, and orders through supported marketplaces, including eBay.

1. Information We Process

When users authorize PAT AUTOSELL, the application may access account identifiers, listing information, inventory details, order information, shipping details, and authorization tokens needed to provide its services.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide authorized marketplace integrations, manage listings and inventory, process orders, and maintain application functionality.

3. Data Sharing

Information may be exchanged with eBay and other authorized service providers as necessary to operate the application's requested features. PAT AUTOSELL does not sell users' personal information.

4. Data Security

PAT AUTOSELL is designed to use OAuth authorization and appropriate safeguards to protect account access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for application functionality, legitimate business purposes, and applicable legal obligations.

6. User Choices

Users may revoke marketplace authorization through their marketplace account settings. Users may also contact the application operator to request information about applicable access or deletion options.
